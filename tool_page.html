Before you upload to Walmart — read this

Walmart has no "upload reviews" button in Seller Center. The legitimate route is Walmart's free Review Syndication Program, which ingests this CSV. Syndicate only reviews you own — the ones customers left on your own store, for the same product. Walmart matches each review to your listing by GTIN/UPC + Item ID + SKU, so those three columns come blank for you to fill.
